About Me

I am an emerging researcher at the intersection of artificial intelligence and medical imaging, driven by the pursuit of diagnostic systems that are accurate, explainable, and robust to real-world distribution shift.

My work draws on convolutional networks, graph neural networks, and Vision Transformer architectures — augmented by hybrid ensemble learning, self-supervised pretraining, and unsupervised domain adaptation. I am equally invested in explainable AI, image forensics, and foundation models, with the long-term goal of translating research prototypes into clinically deployable tools.
